What this page is for

These are sticky paper labels, not 3D printed parts. This page builds a PDF you print on an ordinary inkjet or laser printer, onto a sheet of peel-off address labels — the 30-per-sheet 2.625″ × 1″ kind sold as Avery® 5160 or 8160 and by every generic equivalent.

The point is physical organization: the bin of finished brackets on your shelf gets a label with the product name and SKU on it, so what you pull off the shelf matches what the catalog says it is. You can include the SKU, a quantity and a QR code — toggle those under Settings.

Pick a start position so a part-used sheet is not wasted, add the products you want, then generate. If labels land slightly off the sheet, the alignment offsets and test sheet are in Settings → Label Sheet Alignment — that means your paper printer, not your 3D printer.

This page is entirely optional. Nothing in the catalog, costing or job quoting depends on it. If you only came here for pricing, you can ignore it.
