Printed Parts ({{ p.parts|length }})

Every printed piece that makes up one finished product. A one-piece product has a single part. Values come straight from Bambu Studio, for the whole plate.

No colour bound to the SKU. A SKU is still the recipe, not the colour — {{ p.sku }} is the same part in white or blue. You choose the filament per job on the Job Calculator, and that is where the exact cost and the stock deduction happen.

Filament Cost below is an estimate, not a binding choice. Link a filament pool and this page prices the part from what that colour has actually cost, blended across every brand you buy it from — Hobby Lobby white and Bambu white average together. That number is for this page's estimate only; it never limits which colour a customer can actually get, and the real cost always comes from whatever colour is picked at quote time. Leave it unlinked, or tick Override, to keep typing the figure by hand.
