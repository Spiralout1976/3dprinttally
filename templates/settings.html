Used for any part where Active Labor is left blank. This is recurring per-plate handling time — pulling parts off, deburring, bagging. It is multiplied by every print run. Design time does not belong here.
Share of prints you expect to fail. On a job needing more than one plate this is added as extra print runs. On a job that fits on a single plate it is added as a cost uplift instead, because rounding up to a second full plate would charge 100% for a 10% allowance. 0 disables it entirely.

Custom Design Work

One-time charges. These apply only to jobs marked as custom design work on the Job Cost Calculator — never to a catalog product with a personalization field, and never to a reorder of a design you have already been paid for.

CAD and setup time, billed separately from Labor Rate. Charged once per job from the Design Time log, and deliberately kept out of per-item cost, break-even and the margin ladder so a reorder quotes clean.
Floor for a custom design job. Covers the messaging, file prep and packaging that happen no matter how small the job. Never applied to catalog products. 0 disables it.

Calibration Workflow

  1. Download the calibration PDF.
  2. Print on plain US Letter paper at Actual Size / 100%.
  3. Place it behind a blank label sheet and hold both to a light.
  4. Measure any horizontal/vertical drift in millimeters.
  5. Enter the correction offsets here and print again.
